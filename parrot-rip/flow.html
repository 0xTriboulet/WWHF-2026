parrot-rip.exe — voice → text via the inbox desktop SAPI5 engine

Derived 1:1 from parrot-rip/parrot-rip.c (post-format). Two halves: capture (MME waveIn, 16 kHz PCM into a heap buffer) and engine (in-proc SpInprocRecognizer + dictation grammar fed the captured audio as an ISpStreamFormat stream, so the engine never touches the audio device). Exit codes: 0 utterance transcribed · 1 engine/usage/no-speech · 5 output-file error.
